## VAIDHURYA Investor Deck Narrative Plan

### Audience
Early-stage investors evaluating premium commerce, AI-native consumer products, and India-focused marketplace opportunities.

### Objective
Present VAIDHURYA as a credible, premium, AI-native multi-seller jewelry marketplace built for the fragmented Indian jewelry segment.

### Narrative Arc
1. Establish VAIDHURYA as a premium, AI-native jewelry marketplace.
2. Frame the Indian jewelry market as fragmented, trust-heavy, and poorly personalized online.
3. Show why the product is agentic rather than simply AI-assisted.
4. Explain the buyer and seller product loop.
5. Position the current stage as founder-led product and market validation.
6. Introduce the founding team.
7. Clarify the monetization model.
8. Show a practical technical foundation with Claude and AWS.
9. Close with a fundraising-ready framing and next milestone.

### Slide List
1. Cover
2. Problem + Solution
3. What Makes It Agentic
4. Product Overview
5. Traction
6. Team
7. Business Model
8. Tech Stack + Planned Use of Claude and AWS
9. Fundraising Status

### Source Plan
- Repository README and architecture docs for platform and product direction.
- Founder inputs from the conversation for market framing, team, and product vision.
- No external market-size or traction figures are included because they were not provided.

### Visual System
- Palette centered on velvety emerald, ivory, walnut brown, cream, and white.
- Premium editorial layout with serif headlines, clean body copy, and soft luxury overlays.
- Jewelry photography from the repository used as art-direction plates.

### Asset Plan
- Reuse local jewelry imagery from `public/`.
- Reuse VAIDHURYA logo from `public/vaidhurya-logo.png`.

### Editability Plan
- All meaningful copy remains editable PowerPoint text.
- Photography stays as image layers with translucent overlays for readability.
- No decorative text baked into images.

### Assumptions
- Fundraising is framed as pre-seed without a specific amount because none was provided.
- Traction is presented as product-readiness and early validation stage, not as invented metrics.
